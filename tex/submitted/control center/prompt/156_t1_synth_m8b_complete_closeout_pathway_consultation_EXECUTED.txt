## 1.1 What "M8b" is

M8b is the milestone axis tracking the **PCF-1 §3 dichotomy** -- the empirical
split where the WKB-decay exponent A takes the value 4 for sgn(Δ₂) > 0 vs 3
for sgn(Δ₂) < 0 in degree-2 polynomial continued fractions -- and its
underlying **alien-amplitude / Stokes-multiplier S₂** analytic substrate.

The dichotomy was first observed empirically in PCF-1 v1.3 (Theorem 5,
sharp WKB exponent at d=2 stratified by sgn(Δ₂)). M8a V0 (cascade 127R,
bridge `cb429e1`) explicitly delegates the SUBSTANTIVE structural
resolution of this dichotomy to the M8b axis with annotation
`(STOKES-DICHOTOMY-DELEGATED-TO-M8B)`. M8b is therefore the axis where
the dichotomy has to be either:
  (a) numerically resolved by extracting |S₂| values that respect the
      dichotomy at d=2; OR
  (b) structurally resolved by a Birkhoff–Trjitzinsky-style descendant
      analysis with full Stokes data; OR
  (c) categorically foreclosed (which is what V0 does, by negative-result
      acceptance).

## 1.2 The current M8b V0 closure (bridge `74c5630`, 2026-05-09)

M8b V0 was closed via **numerical-foreclosure-by-residual-acceptance** at
the d=2 PCF Stokes-multiplier |S₂| scale, with annotation:

## 1.3 What "complete closeout" of M8b would mean (working definition)

The M8b V0 closure is a **negative-result closure** at the cascade level.
"Complete closeout" of M8b -- which we will alias as **M8b V1** for the
purpose of this consultation -- would, in principle, require lifting one
or more of the carry-forward annotations:

  Carry-forward 1: `NUMERICAL-FORECLOSURE`
    - V0 closes the laptop-feasible numerical channel by residual
      acceptance.
    - "V1" would replace residual acceptance with either:
      (1.a) positive |S₂| extraction at d=2 via a methodology not yet
            surveyed (e.g., the U2 uncharted-quadrant: small-(N,M)
            subtracted Padé), OR
      (1.b) a structural argument that subsumes the numerical extraction
            (e.g., Birkhoff–Trjitzinsky descendant analysis providing a
            closed-form |S₂| at d=2), OR
      (1.c) an upper-bound proof on |S₂| compatible with the residual-
            floor observation, supplying a mathematically rigorous
            "below resolution floor" statement to replace methodological
            acceptance.

  Carry-forward 2: `d≥3-CAVEAT-CARRIED-FORWARD`
    - V0 closes only at d=2; d≥3 is forward-pointed and not blocking.
    - "V1" would require either:
      (2.a) extending the numerical-foreclosure framework to d≥3 (i.e.,
            running 4-lens harvest at d=3, d=4 at minimum), OR
      (2.b) a structural argument addressing why d≥3 is not separately
            reducible (i.e., why the d=2 foreclosure subsumes d≥3), OR
      (2.c) dispatching the P-009 caveat active variant v1 (currently
            `NOT_YET_DISPATCHED`) and either confirming or refuting the
            d≥3 binding-window framing.

  Carry-forward 3: U2 uncharted-quadrant (forward-pointer)
    - The 4th of 4 methodological quadrants -- small-(N,M) subtracted
      Padé -- is untested. R2's §5 amendment specification flags this
      as a future pipeline-survey forward pointer.
    - "V1" would test U2 and either:
      (3.a) confirm foreclosure-in-U2, strengthening V0 by surveying
            all 4 quadrants, OR
      (3.b) discover a non-foreclosed quadrant, which would convert
            M8b V0's foreclosure-by-residual-acceptance into a
            partial-foreclosure-with-positive-extraction.

  Residual annotation: M8a → M8b dichotomy delegation
    - M8a V0 explicitly delegates the PCF-1 §3 A∈{3,4} dichotomy to
      M8b. M8b V0 closes this delegation only at the numerical scale.
    - "V1" would provide structural resolution of the dichotomy
      (item 1.b above is the strongest path here).
